Wilson Lines in General Representations

This project started when I moved to Parma to join Carlo Meneghelli's group. Together with Carlo, Rudolfs Treilis, and Michele Savi, we explored Wilson lines beyond the fundamental representation and the new defect operators and structures that emerge in their spectrum.

Interestingly, we discovered that, in sufficiently rich representations, many of these operators have the quantum numbers of the displacement supermultiplet. This means that they can be used as deformations of the defect theory: by computing their beta functions, we showed that these deformations are marginally relevant.

This opens interesting questions about the renormalization-group flow they generate. Together with Carlo, Rudolfs, and Michele, we continue to work on understanding these exotic operators.
