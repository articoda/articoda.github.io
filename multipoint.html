Multipoint Correlators for the Maldacena-Wilson line

I started this project together with Julien Barrat and Giulia Peveri during our time as PhD students in the Research and Training Group 2575 - Rethinking Quantum Field Theory at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin. What started as a side project developed into a full research program, giving rise to two papers published in JHEP and contributing to three PhD theses.

Maldacena-Wilson lines describe heavy charged particles in N=4 Supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory. Their preserved super-conformal symmetry implies that its excitations form a 1-dimensional CFT. Our original scope was to study the first non-trivial correlation function, the 4-point correlator, up to next-to-next-to-leading order at weak coupling; it was then extended to 5- and 6- point once we realized that our method could be applied beyond the simplest multipoint correlator.

We called the method we developed during the project perturbative bootstrap: it consists in applying all non-perturbative constraints coming from super-conformal Ward identities, conformal symmetry, and crossing, to then compute the remaining necessary information via Feynman diagrams. The perturbative bootstrap approach allowed us to push beyond state-of-the-art results as it heavily reduced the amount of diagrams we needed to compute.

Together with Yinxuan Xu , Julien and I applied the perturbative bootstrap to correlation functions including one bulk and two defect operators, with results both at weak and strong coupling.
