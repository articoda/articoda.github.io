Theoretical Physics and Other Things I Like

Here you can find my work on perturbative Quantum Field Theory, defect CFT, operator mixing, and more. My research combines conformal symmetry and Feynman-integral methods to extract spectra and correlators, with applications to heavy particles in general representations and defects at finite temperature.
Also, you can check some of my projects on the climate emergency.
